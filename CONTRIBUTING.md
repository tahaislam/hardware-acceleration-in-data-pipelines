# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this repository.

## What We Are Looking For

- **Real-world integration experience:** If you have accelerated a data pipeline stage in production, your experience — including what went wrong — is valuable. Case studies, even anonymized, are welcome.
- **Vendor-neutral architectural analysis:** Trade-off discussions that do not depend on a specific vendor's toolchain or product.
- **Diagram improvements:** Clearer or more accurate visual representations of the concepts in the framework.
- **Additional worked examples:** Non-proprietary examples that demonstrate acceleration trade-offs in different workload types.

## What We Are Not Looking For

- Vendor-specific tutorials or product endorsements.
- Micro-benchmark results without end-to-end context.
- Low-level FPGA/GPU programming guides (these exist elsewhere and are well-maintained).
- Content that reads like marketing material.

## Guidelines

- Write at a Staff/Principal engineering level. Assume the reader is experienced.
- Distinguish clearly between facts, opinions, and estimates. See the framework documents for examples of this tone.
- If you include numbers (throughput, latency, cost), state the conditions under which they were measured.
- Diagrams should be committed as SVG with source files (draw.io, Excalidraw, etc.) for future editing.

## Process

1. Open an issue describing what you intend to contribute.
2. Fork the repository and create a branch.
3. Submit a pull request with a clear description of the change.

Thank you for contributing to better engineering decisions.
